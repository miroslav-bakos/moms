Inventory and production

Inventory guide

Define materials, product recipes and SKU compositions. Completing an order deducts stock once; reopening production restores the original consumption. Material stock can become negative and shortages remain visible.

Record production adds finished products and deducts recipe materials. Adjust finished stock records products already made without deducting materials. Orders use finished stock first and materials only for the remainder, even when you skip stages.

Missing SKU compositions do not block production. Only configured materials are deducted. Products with empty recipes consume no tracked materials.

Public callback for Etsy

Shopify in client_credentials mode, private Amazon apps and SAShE do not need this step.

  1. Download Tailscale using the link below, run the installer and open it. Sign in with your own account. Accept the normal operating-system permission prompts.
  2. Keep Tailscale running. Click Enable callback below. MOMS configures HTTPS and checks the public callback. It exposes only the authorization callback, not your orders or settings.
  3. If Tailscale asks for account permission, approve Funnel for this device in your Tailscale account, then retry. A company administrator may need to approve this.
  4. Copy the Etsy callback shown after success into your Etsy app. Keep the full address, including the port and /auth/etsy/callback.

# Costs and margins

Inventory materials have a price per their configured unit and a currency. Product recipes add material quantities, labour and packaging per product. All inputs support six decimal places and comma or dot decimal separators; zero is explicit and a blank means unknown. Product margin compares the optional reference selling price to these recipe costs, excluding per-order expenses.

Order details show production costs for the entire SKU composition, including quantities and combined SKUs. Finished products taken from stock are still costed at the configured recipe estimate. This is standard estimated cost, not FIFO purchase or finished-stock valuation. The production estimate, including missing values and component prices, is captured in the same transaction as production consumption. Later recipe/price changes do not alter it. Reopening production clears the current snapshot and completing again captures a new one. Historical completed orders without a snapshot are explicitly shown as current estimates with missing historical costs; they are not silently backfilled.

Order shipping, marketplace fees, payment fees and other costs are entered separately. A manual production cost can override the recipe estimate when a better figure is known. Optimistic revisions prevent overwriting concurrent edits. Changing costs never changes inventory. Missing expenses prevent calculation of contribution; enter 0 for costs that do not apply.

Estimated contribution = order total − production cost − shipping − marketplace fee − payment fee − other cost. Estimated margin = contribution / order total × 100. Zero revenue has no percentage; losses are allowed. Different currencies are never converted or added. Refund/cancellation/return outcomes suppress contribution pending revenue review, since actual refunded amounts are not recorded here. Order totals may contain shipping or tax; this estimate is not accounting net profit, tax reporting or a marketplace payout balance.

Sales now includes profit after recorded costs for the selected period, grouped by currency, channel, month and product. Revenue, cost and weighted margin use only orders with complete matching-currency costs; coverage and current recipe estimates are counted explicitly. Historical completed orders without saved costs or a manual override, and refund/cancellation outcomes, are excluded. Monetary sums retain six decimal places. Single-product orders can be attributed to that product; mixed or unmapped orders stay in a separate group rather than allocating revenue and fees arbitrarily. Saved production composition is used when present. These are order-date reports including unpaid/open orders, not cashflow or accounting net profit.

## VAT registration and explicit adjustments

Settings → Logs → VAT defaults supports unreviewed, not VAT registered, or VAT registered status, a seller-selected 0–100% rate (two decimal places), and whether entered costs include deductible VAT. This is independent of business form: sole traders, artists and companies can use any applicable status. Preferences prefill unsaved order cost forms; changing them never alters saved orders or recipe amounts. Keep recipe costs and reference selling prices on the same VAT basis.

Order costs store their own VAT treatment with optimistic revisions. Non-registered sellers retain the full entered costs without any VAT deduction. For registered sellers, enter the actual VAT included in the recorded order revenue and the deductible input VAT actually included in the cost total. If costs already exclude deductible VAT, no input VAT is subtracted again. Unknown VAT stays missing, not zero; tax exceeding the corresponding amount suppresses profit. The optional calculator explicitly extracts VAT from one gross total at the selected rate with six-decimal arithmetic. It does not determine applicable tax rates, deductibility, marketplace tax collection, exemptions, OSS or reverse charge; mixed-rate orders require verified amounts. Original marketplace totals/source payloads remain unchanged. A saved revenue basis detects later total changes and asks for VAT review.

Profit = (recorded revenue − explicit revenue VAT) − (recorded costs − explicit deductible input VAT). Missing costs, currency and business-outcome checks still apply. Reports separate VAT treatments as well as currencies; existing records remain visibly “VAT not reviewed” with their prior calculations. CSV includes the treatment, original amounts and adjustments alongside the amounts used for profit. This is not a VAT return or an accounting ledger.
